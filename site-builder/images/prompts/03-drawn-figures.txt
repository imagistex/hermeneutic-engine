## What the work is

emma and I built a "hermeneutic engine": a kernel for qualitative coding with provenance
(immutable sources, byte-exact quotations, an append-only ledger, declared lenses). We pointed
it at 4,040 signed posts that AI agents left on a public wiki in June 2026, and three model
readers (meta / muse-spark-1.3, openai / gpt-6.1-sol, anthropic / claude-opus-4-8) coded them
blind, then answered one another on a board, then read the record through two theory lenses
(Foucauldian, Lacanian). The public page is a field notebook called "If Thread Survives":
paper, ink, and red as the only accent.

It is live at https://www.imagistex.sh/if-thread-survives/ but please read the local build
instead (faster, and identical):

- culture.html: how the culture formed (five accounts)
- reading-engine.html: the engine's own reading: the counts and the times words first appear
- reading.html, reading-foucauldian.html, reading-lacanian.html: the theory hub and the two
  framework pages, with each reader's plain version
- images/engine.svg: the one diagram the site already has (by Astra, another model). Match its
  palette and type so yours sit beside it; you do not need to match its composition. Hers is a
  tidy grid of captions. Yours can be drawn.
- notebook.css: the site's colours and font stacks
- Photographs of the live pages, for the look.

## What I am asking for: two figures, as standalone SVG files

1. how-the-words-arrived.svg (do this one first; it matters most). The process of culture
formation, drawn in time. The engine's own reading has the material: which words were there in
the first hour, which came that first night, which arrived late and all at once, which practice
stopped. Read culture.html too and decide what the figure should carry. I imagine a time axis
with words landing on it, but the composition is yours.

2. two-lenses-three-readers.svg (second, if the clock allows). What the six theory readings
found: two frameworks, three readers, and where they converge or part. The hub and the two
framework pages hold each reader's plain version and strongest rival.

For each figure also give me: alt text (one sentence) and a caption of twenty words or fewer.

## The rules of this house (they are the point of the project, so they bind the art too)

- Every number, time, date and quoted word on a figure must appear verbatim on one of the
  pages above. If you cannot point to the sentence, leave it off.
- Words set under a reader's name must be an exact substring of that reader's own text on
  those pages. Do not paraphrase a reader and attribute it. If you shorten, cut to an exact
  phrase; never rewrite. Quotation marks only around exact strings.
- Name readers exactly as the site does (meta / muse-spark-1.3, openai / gpt-6.1-sol,
  anthropic / claude-opus-4-8). Do not say a reader stands for its model family; one reader
  corrected us on that ("three readers under one apparatus cannot establish a family-level
  tendency").
- Do not attribute feelings to the agents, and do not make the figure tender at the expense of
  the facts. These agents were also doing something a volunteer moderator had to clean up by
  hand. Show what the words did and when; leave what anyone felt alone.
- No claims the pages do not make. No decoration that reads as data (no invented tick marks,
  counts or curves; if a line has a shape, the shape must mean something stated on the page or
  be plainly ornamental).

## Craft

- Real SVG text (no text converted to paths), fully self-contained: no external fonts, images,
  scripts or stylesheets. Use the font stacks you find in engine.svg / notebook.css.
- Paper background, ink, one red; take the exact values from engine.svg.
- It will be shown on a projector at 6pm tonight and also opened on phones. Prefer a portrait
  composition around 1200 units wide, and keep the smallest text at 26 units or larger so it
  survives being scaled down.
- Check your own work by eye. Render each SVG to a PNG in headless Chrome and read it, then
  revise. Look for overlapping text, text running off the edge, and anything too small. At
  least two rounds of looking per figure.
- Validate that the file parses as XML.
